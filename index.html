About

I am an Associate Professor of Political Science at HSE University, specializing in comparative politics and authoritarian regimes. My research examines democratic backsliding, political polarization, and strategies of authoritarian survival through both quantitative analysis and in-depth case studies.

Currently, I'm completing a book manuscript titled "The Perfect Storm: Aspiring Autocrats and Democratic Breakdown in Turkey, Hungary and Venezuela", which investigates contemporary autocratization processes through an actor-centric framework. My work has been published in leading journals including Democratization and Energy Research & Social Science, and recognized with the Polity Prize for Best Article.

I hold a PhD in Political Science from the University of Connecticut and have over a decade of teaching experience in comparative politics, research methods, and democratization studies. At HSE University, I've received multiple teaching awards including Best Lecturer (2018, 2020) and Best Academic Advisor (2019).
