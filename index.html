Research

My work examines the dynamics of democratization, authoritarian resilience, and political polarization using mixed-methods approaches. Current projects include:

  • Book in Progress: "The Perfect Storm: Aspiring Autocrats and Democratic Breakdown in Turkey, Hungary, and Venezuela" (to be submitted in 2023).
  • Special Journal Issue: Editing "Nations Divided: Societal Polarization and Political Leadership" (under review at Democratization).
  • Recent Publications:
    • “United We Stand, Divided We Rule” (2021, Democratization)
    • “Elite Survival Strategies and Authoritarian Reversal in Turkey” (2018, Polity – Winner of the 2019 Polity Prize)
